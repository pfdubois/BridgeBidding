41. Summary Tables¶

This chapter collects the summary charts for the chapters on responding to openings.

Summary of 1N Responses¶

Here is a summary of the responses to a 1N Opening, classified by hand type, and hand strength.

Balanced or Semi-Balanced Hands¶

This chart is for the standard 15-17 HCP 1N opener. System On means that transfers and the minor relay are on. NMF means they are not, use New Minor Forcing.

Note

4NT is always quantitative after 1NT (never Blackwood / RKC). Use 4♣ Gerber to ask for aces instead.

Unbalanced Hands¶

One 5+ Card Major M (no 4-card other major)¶

Weak (0-7 HCP): Jacoby transfer (2♦ → hearts, 2♥ → spades) then pass.

Invitational (8-9 HCP):

  • With a 5-card major, transfer, then bid 2NT after opener accepts.

  • With 6 (or more), transfer, then bid 3M.

Game Force (10-15 HCP):

  • With a 5-card major, transfer, then bid 3NT.

  • With 6 (or more), transfer, then bid 4M. See Texas Transfers.

Slam Interest (16+ HCP):

  • With a 5-card major, transfer, then bid 3NT.

  • With 6 (or more), transfer then quantitative 4M.

** Super-Accepts (Advanced)**:

  • Opener may jump to 3M with a maximum (17 HCP) and good trump support (See Advanced Notrump for variations).

Follow-ups after Opener Accepts the Transfer

After 1N – 2t – 2M:

  • New minor at 3-level = game force with a second suit. Could be strong.

  • 3M = invitational with 6+ trumps.

  • 3NT = to play (opener can correct to 4M with a fit).

  • 4♣ = Gerber.

  • 4M = 6 (or more) trumps. See Texas Transfers, recommended. If playing Texas, this sequence is a mild slam try (“slow shows”).

  • 4NT = quantitative.

One Four-Card Major¶

Use Stayman but NOT if your hand is 4=3=3=3 or 3=4=4=3. The hand should be invitational or better, unless weak, short in clubs, intending to pass opener’s rebid regardless.

Bid 2♣︎; the opener rebids a 4 (or more)-card major, or bids 2♦︎ to deny one. If opener rebids your major, revalue your hand, then:

  • Raise to 3M with an invitational hand.

  • Raise to 4M with 10+ points.

  • If you have slam interest (say 16+) you can power-raise by bidding 3 of the other major (3W!), which invites opener to start bidding a control.

If opener doesn’t show a fit then bid NT at an appropriate level as in the no-major case.

Both Majors (4-4, 5-4)¶

If weak (0-7 HCP), transfer to a five-card major and pass, unless Garbage Stayman applies (4=4=4=1 or 4=4=5=0).

Otherwise start with Stayman 2♣ (artificial, asks for a 4-card major). Opener replies:

  • 2♦ = no 4-card major

  • 2♥ = 4+ hearts (may have spades)

  • 2♠ = 4+ spades (denies 4 hearts)

Then invite or raise to game in a discovered fit, or bid 2NT/3NT if no fit.

Optional: With a game-forcing hand, after a 2♦ denial, use Smolen.

5-5 Majors¶

You may have an agreement at the 3-level showing these hands. If not, which is the default:

  • Weak: Transfer to the better major then pass

  • Invitational: Transfer to hearts then bid 2♠.

  • Game forcing: Transfer to spades then bid 3♥︎.

Quiz: How do we know the responder is 5-5 in the majors in these cases?

Answer: he did not bid Stayman.

Long Minor (no 4-card major)¶

Weak (0-7 HCP only): 2♠ (Minor Relay):

  • Forces opener to bid 3♣. Responder then bids pass or corrects to 3♦ (to play).

Invitational (8-9 HCP):

  • Direct 3♣ or 3♦ (natural invite). If you are playing the 3-level bids as something else, you’ll have another way.

Game Force (10-15 HCP):

  • Minor Relay, then bid 3NT.

Slam Interest (16+ HCP):

  • Bid 2♠︎ (Minor Relay), then bid 3♥︎ to show interest in a club slam, or 3♠︎ to show interest in a diamond slam. This is part of the Minor Relay convention.

Quick Reference¶

These are the most common standard responses:

  • Pass: weak

  • 2♣: Stayman (4+ major(s) or special weak hands)

  • 2♦: Jacoby transfer to hearts (5+ hearts)

  • 2♥: Jacoby transfer to spades (5+spades)

  • 2♠: Minor Relay (6+ minor, usually weak)

  • 2NT: balanced, invitational (8-9)

  • 3NT: balanced game (10-15)

  • 4NT: quantitative slam try

  • 4♦/4♥: Texas transfer (optional, game force with 6+ major)

Recommended 1N Structure for Intermediates¶

Here is what I recommend for intermediates. You’ll need partnership agreement.

  • Play the major transfers and Texas transfers, and Minor Relay.

  • Play the 1N – 3x bids as natural and invitational.

Do not try to get to five of a minor; the basic decision is whether to go past 3N or not. Once you go past 3N, you’re going to really hope you can bid the slam because 5m is usually a bad outcome (especially in matchpoints) if 3N is making. The minor relay slam try has the virtue of letting opener call it off with 3N or 5m depending on his hand; or he can control-bid or ask for Aces.

Warning

A great many players, some with considerable experience, are misinformed about sequences like 1N – 2♣ – 2M – 4N and 1N – 2T(transfer) – 2M – 4N. These are all quantitative. Direct skeptics to any good book or professional web page on 2/1. Do not let an allegedly more experienced partner bully you on this.

Responses To 1M¶

These summarize Responding To Major Openings.

Each table has the same rows in the same order, so you can compare the situations.

Unpassed Hand, No Interference¶

Responder’s action

Meaning

Pass

Fewer than 6 HCP; with some hands a light simple raise to 2M.

Simple raise

2M: 3 (or more) trump, 6-10-.

Limit raise, 3-card

1N (forcing), then 3M: three trump, 10-11.

Limit raise, 4-card

3M: 4 (or more) trump, 10-11.

Game-forcing raise

2N! (Jacoby 2NT): 4 (or more) trump, game forcing. With three trump, a 2/1 bid (or 1♠), then support.

Splinter

A double jump in a new suit (3♠ over 1♥; 4♣ or 4♦ over 1M; 4♥ over 1♠): game-going raise with a singleton or void. Alerted.

Preemptive raise

4M: weak, 5 (or more) trump and shortness.

1N

Forcing for one round, 6-11. Need not be balanced. Announced.

New suit at the 1 level

1♠ over 1♥: 4 (or more) spades, 6 (or more) HCP, forcing.

New suit at the 2 level

2/1, forcing to game: an opening hand; 2♥ over 1♠ promises 5 (or more) hearts, a minor 4 (or more) (rarely 3).

Jump shift

3♣ or 3♦: six cards, invitational, not forcing. For other jump shifts see All About Jump-Shifts.

2N

Jacoby 2NT (see game-forcing raise).

3N

Not applicable.

Double

Not applicable.

Redouble

Not applicable.

Cue bid of their suit

Not applicable.
Passed Hand, No Interference¶

Responder’s action

Meaning

Pass

Fewer than 6 HCP. With four trump, or three trump to a high honor (A, K, or Q), and some shape, you can choose to raise to 2M; with five trump, consider the preemptive 4M.

Simple raise

2M: 3 (or more) trump, 6-10-.

Limit raise, 3-card

2♣! (Reverse Drury): limit raise, 3 (or more) trump, forcing.

Limit raise, 4-card

2♣! (Reverse Drury), the same bid.

Game-forcing raise

2♣! Drury (the passed hand got better with the fit).

Splinter

A double jump in a new suit, when the fit has made the hand game-going.

Preemptive raise

3M: weak, four trump. 4M: weak, 5 (or more) trump and shortness.

1N

Not forcing: 6-9 HCP, no support. With 10 HCP and no fit, you may downgrade to 1N.

New suit at the 1 level

1♠ over 1♥: 4 (or more) spades, 6 (or more) HCP, not forcing.

New suit at the 2 level

An invitational hand, not forcing. 2♣ is Drury, not natural: with only five clubs, bid 3♣ if they are good enough, otherwise 1N.

Jump shift

3♣ or 3♦: six cards, invitational, not forcing. For other jump shifts see All About Jump-Shifts.

2N

11-12, balanced, no fit, not forcing.

3N

Not applicable.

Double

Not applicable.

Redouble

Not applicable.

Cue bid of their suit

Not applicable.
After A Takeout Double: 1M (X)¶

Responder’s action

Meaning

Pass

Less than 6 HCP, not suitable for a preemptive raise.

Simple raise

2M, the same as without the double.

Limit raise, 3-card

Redouble, then show the support.

Limit raise, 4-card

2N! (Jordan 2NT): 4 (or more) trump, limit raise or better.

Game-forcing raise

Not applicable. (Jordan 2NT is forcing.)

Splinter

As without the double.

Preemptive raise

3M is weak, four trump; 4M is five or more trump.

1N

Not forcing, 6-9 HCP, no fit.

New suit at the 1 level

1♠ over 1♥: 6 (or more) HCP, forcing one round; some hands with 10+ HCP redouble instead.

New suit at the 2 level

6-9, 2/1 is off, not forcing.

Jump shift

Weak (e.g. 1♥ (X) 3♣: six clubs, weak).

2N

Jordan 2NT (see limit raise, 4-card).

3N

Not applicable.

Double

Not applicable.

Redouble

10+ HCP, denies four trump; forcing to two of opener’s suit (Going For Blood).

Cue bid of their suit

Not applicable.
After An Overcall: 1M (overcall)¶

Responder’s action

Meaning

Pass

Weak hands. Also pass with a trump stack in their suit, and let opener reopen.

Simple raise

2M, 3 (or more) trump, 6-10-; Axxx of trump and a ruffing value is also enough.

Limit raise, 3-card

Cue bid of their suit: limit raise or better.

Limit raise, 4-card

The same cue bid. By partnership agreement, 2N when the cue bid would force to game, as in 1♥ (2♠).

Game-forcing raise

Cue bid, then game. 1♥ (2♠) 3♠ is game forcing.

Splinter

A jump cue bid.

Preemptive raise

A jump raise to 3M is preemptive, usually four trump. 4M: weak, 5 (or more) trump.

1N

1♥ (1♠) 1N only: typically shows a spade stopper but not enough values for 2N, say 8-9 HCP.

New suit at the 1 level

Not applicable.

New suit at the 2 level

10+ points and a five-card suit. Forcing, but not game forcing.

Jump shift

Weak (preemptive).

2N

Natural: invitational, balanced, a stopper in their suit. Can be passed.

3N

To play, with stoppers in their suit.

Double

Negative double (through 2♠): 6 points at the one level, 8 to 10 at the two level.

Redouble

Not applicable.

Cue bid of their suit

Limit raise or better in opener’s major. A jump cue bid is a splinter.

Responses To 1m¶

These summarize Responding To Minor Openings.

Each table has the same rows in the same order, so you can compare the situations. Where the minors chapter is silent, an entry follows the majors tables and says “as after a major”.

Unpassed Hand, No Interference¶

Responder’s action

Meaning

Pass

Fewer than 6 HCP, not suitable for a preemptive raise.

Simple raise

None. With 6-10- and a fit, bid a suit at the one level or 1N.

Limit raise or better

2m! (Inverted Minors): 10+ HCP, 5 (or more) trump (or four good diamonds), no four-card major. Unlimited, forcing one round, alerted.

Game-forcing raise

2m!, then bid stoppers up the line; responder can go directly to 3N.

Splinter

Not applicable.

Preemptive raise

3m!: 0-6 HCP, 5 (or more) trump.

Jump to 4m

Slam try (a double jump): seriously interested in 6m.

1N

6-10, natural, not forcing; need not be balanced. Also with exactly 10 and no major or fit.

New suit at the 1 level

4 (or more) cards, 6 (or more) HCP, forcing. Over 1♣, a four-card major before diamonds unless game forcing (Walsh).

New suit at the 2 level

1♦ – 2♣: forcing to game, 13+ HCP, 5 (or more) clubs.

Jump shift

1♦ – 3♣: 6 (or more) clubs, invitational. Other jump shifts are weak and alerted.

2N

11-12-, no four-card major, invitational.

3N

Balanced, 13+ HCP, game forcing, stoppers in the other suits, no four-card major.

Double

Not applicable.

Redouble

Not applicable.

Not applicable.

After An Overcall: 1m (overcall)¶

Responder’s action

Meaning

Pass

Weak hands. Also pass with a trump stack in their suit, and let opener reopen (as after a major).

Simple raise

2m: 6-10-, 5 (or more) trump.

Limit raise or better

Cue bid of their suit at the two level.

Game-forcing raise

Cue bid, then game (as after a major).

Splinter

Not applicable.

Preemptive raise

A jump raise to 3m.

Jump to 4m

Slam try.

1N

A stopper in their suit, not enough for 2N, say 8-9 HCP (as after a major).

New suit at the 1 level

Natural, 6 (or more) HCP, forcing.

New suit at the 2 level

Natural, forcing, 10+ HCP. 1♦ – 2♣ shows 5 (or more) clubs.

Jump shift

Weak (preemptive) (as after a major).

2N

Natural: invitational, balanced, a stopper in their suit (as after a major).

3N

To play, with stoppers in their suit (as after a major).

Double

Negative double; see negative doubles.

Redouble

Not applicable.

Cue bid of their suit
