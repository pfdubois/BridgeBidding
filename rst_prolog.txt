..
   Point and length abbreviations, used everywhere in the sources.

   Naming scheme:
     |Normore|  "N or more"  (points or cards), e.g. |10ormore|, |5ormore|
     |badN|     "a bad N"    (top of a range, slightly less than N), e.g. 6-|bad10|

   Rendering: currently the number followed by a superscript + or -.
   To change the style everywhere, edit only the two patterns below, e.g.
     superscript:   10\ :sup:`+`
     plain:         10+
     words:         10 (or more)
   The "\ " (backslash space) joins the superscript to the number without a gap.
   When a new number is needed, add a line in the same pattern.
