Summary of 1N Responses 
-----------------------

Here is a summary of the responses to a 1N Opening, classified by hand type,
and hand strength.

Balanced or Semi-Balanced Hands
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. index::
   single: 1N; summary of responses

This chart is for the standard 15-17 HCP 1N opener. System On means that transfers
and the minor relay are on. NMF means they are not, use :ref:`New Minor Forcing <NMF>`.

.. table:: Balanced Openings

   +-----+------------+-------------+
   |HCP  | Opening Bid| System On?  |
   +=====+============+=============+
   +12-14|1x then 1N  | NMF         |
   +-----+------------+-------------+
   |15-17|1N          | Yes         |
   +-----+------------+-------------+
   |18-19|1m then 2N  | NMF         |
   +-----+------------+-------------+
   |20-21|2N          | Yes         |
   +-----+------------+-------------+
   |22-24|2♣ then 2N  | Yes         |
   +-----+------------+-------------+
   |25-27|2♣ then 3N  | Yes         |
   +-----+------------+-------------+
   |28-30|2♣ then 4N  | Yes         |
   +-----+------------+-------------+

.. note::
   4NT is always quantitative after 1NT (never Blackwood / RKC).
   Use 4♣ Gerber to ask for aces instead.
   
Unbalanced Hands
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

One 5+ Card Major M (no 4-card other major)
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

**Weak (0-7 HCP)**: Jacoby transfer (2♦ → hearts, 2♥ → spades) then pass.

**Invitational (8-9 HCP)**:

* With a 5-card major, transfer, then bid 2NT after opener accepts.
* With |6ormore|, transfer, then bid 3M. 

**Game Force (10-15 HCP)**:

* With a 5-card major, transfer, then bid 3NT.
* With |6ormore|, transfer, then bid 4M. See :ref:`Texas Transfers <Texas>`.

**Slam Interest (16+ HCP)**:

* With a 5-card major, transfer, then bid 3NT.
* With |6ormore|, transfer then quantitative 4M.

** Super-Accepts (Advanced)**:

* Opener may jump to 3M with a maximum (17 HCP) and good trump support
  (See :ref:`Advanced Notrump <AdvancedNT>` for variations).

.. rubric:: Follow-ups after Opener Accepts the Transfer

After 1N – 2t – 2M:

* New minor at 3-level = game force with a second suit.  Could be strong.
* 3M = invitational with 6+ trumps. 
* 3NT = to play (opener can correct to 4M with a fit). 
* 4♣ = Gerber. 
* 4M = |6ormore| trumps. See :ref:`Texas Transfers <Texas>`, recommended.
  If playing Texas, this sequence is a mild slam try ("slow shows").
* 4NT = quantitative.

One Four-Card Major
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Use :ref:`Stayman <Stayman>` but NOT if your hand is 4=3=3=3 or 3=4=4=3.
The hand should be invitational or better, unless weak, short in clubs, intending to 
pass opener's rebid regardless.

Bid 2♣︎; the opener rebids a |4ormore|-card major, or bids 2♦︎ to deny one. 
If opener rebids your major, revalue your hand, then:

* Raise to 3M with an invitational hand.
* Raise to 4M with 10+ points.
* If you have slam interest (say 16+) you can power-raise by bidding 3 of the other 
  major (3W!), which invites opener to start bidding a :term:`control`.

If opener doesn't show a fit then bid NT at an appropriate level as in the no-major case.


Both Majors (4-4, 5-4)
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

If weak (0-7 HCP), transfer to a five-card major and pass, 
unless :ref:`Garbage Stayman <GarbageStayman>` applies (4=4=4=1 or 4=4=5=0).

Otherwise start with Stayman 2♣ (artificial, asks for a 4-card major).
Opener replies:

* 2♦ = no 4-card major 
* 2♥ = 4+ hearts (may have spades) 
* 2♠ = 4+ spades (denies 4 hearts)

Then invite or raise to game in a discovered fit, or bid
2NT/3NT if no fit. 

Optional: With a game-forcing hand, after a 2♦ denial, use :ref:`Smolen <Smolen>`.

5-5 Majors 
^^^^^^^^^^

You may have an agreement at the 3-level showing these hands. If not, which is the 
default:

* Weak: Transfer to the better major then pass
* Invitational: Transfer to hearts then bid 2♠.
* Game forcing: Transfer to spades then bid 3♥︎.

Quiz: How do we know the responder is 5-5 in the majors in these cases? 

Answer: he did not bid Stayman.

Long Minor (no 4-card major)
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

**Weak (0-7 HCP only)**: 2♠ (:ref:`Minor Relay <minor_relay>`):

* Forces opener to bid 3♣. Responder then bids pass or corrects to 3♦ (to play).

**Invitational (8-9 HCP)**: 

* Direct 3♣ or 3♦ (natural invite).
  If you are playing the 3-level bids as something else, you'll have another way.

**Game Force (10-15 HCP)**: 

* :ref:`Minor Relay <minor_relay>`, then bid 3NT.

**Slam Interest (16+ HCP)**: 

* Bid 2♠︎ (:ref:`Minor Relay <minor_relay>`), then bid 3♥︎ to show interest in a club slam, 
  or 3♠︎ to show interest in a diamond slam.  This is part of the Minor Relay convention.

Quick Reference
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

These are the most common standard responses:

* Pass: weak 
* 2♣: Stayman (4+ major(s) or special weak hands) 
* 2♦: Jacoby transfer to hearts (5+ hearts) 
* 2♥: Jacoby transfer to spades (5+spades) 
* 2♠: Minor Relay (6+ minor, usually weak) 
* 2NT: balanced, invitational (8-9) 
* 3NT: balanced game (10-15) 
* 4NT: quantitative slam try
* 4♦/4♥: Texas transfer (optional, game force with 6+ major)

Recommended 1N Structure for Intermediates
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Here is what I recommend for intermediates.  You'll need partnership agreement.

* Play the major transfers and Texas transfers, and
  :ref:`Minor Relay <Minor_Relay>`.
* Play the 1N – 3x bids as natural and invitational.
  
Do not try to get to five of a minor; the basic decision is whether to go past
3N or not.  Once you go past 3N, you're going to really hope you can bid the
slam because 5m is usually a bad outcome (especially in matchpoints) if 3N is
making. The minor relay slam try has the virtue of letting opener call it off
with 3N or 5m depending on his hand; or he can control-bid or ask for Aces.   
  
.. warning::

   A great many players, some with considerable experience, are misinformed about
   sequences like 1N – 2♣ – 2M – 4N and 1N – 2T(transfer) – 2M – 4N. These are 
   all quantitative. Direct skeptics to any good book or professional web page on 2/1. 
   Do not let an allegedly more experienced partner bully you on this.

Responses To 1M
---------------

These summarize :doc:`Responding To Major Openings <MajorOpenings>`.

Each table has the same rows in the same order, so you can compare the situations.

.. list-table:: Unpassed Hand, No Interference
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 75

   * - Responder's action
     - Meaning
   * - Pass
     - Fewer than 6 HCP; with some hands a light simple raise to 2M.
   * - Simple raise
     - 2M: |3ormore| trump, 6-10\ :sup:`-`.
   * - Limit raise, 3-card
     - 1N (forcing), then 3M: three trump, 10-11.
   * - Limit raise, 4-card
     - 3M: |4ormore| trump, 10-11.
   * - Game-forcing raise
     - 2N! (Jacoby 2NT): |4ormore| trump, game forcing. With three trump, a 2/1 bid (or 1♠), then support.
   * - Splinter
     - A double jump in a new suit (3♠ over 1♥; 4♣ or 4♦ over 1M; 4♥ over 1♠): game-going raise with a singleton or void. Alerted.
   * - Preemptive raise
     - 4M: weak, |5ormore| trump and shortness.
   * - 1N
     - Forcing for one round, 6-11. Need not be balanced. Announced.
   * - New suit at the 1 level
     - 1♠ over 1♥: |4ormore| spades, |6ormore| HCP, forcing.
   * - New suit at the 2 level
     - 2/1, forcing to game: an opening hand; 2♥ over 1♠ promises |5ormore| hearts, a minor |4ormore| (rarely 3).
   * - Jump shift
     - 3♣ or 3♦: six cards, invitational, not forcing. For other jump shifts see :ref:`Jump_Shifts`.
   * - 2N
     - Jacoby 2NT (see game-forcing raise).
   * - 3N
     - Not applicable.
   * - Double
     - Not applicable.
   * - Redouble
     - Not applicable.
   * - Cue bid of their suit
     - Not applicable.

.. list-table:: Passed Hand, No Interference
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 75

   * - Responder's action
     - Meaning
   * - Pass
     - Fewer than 6 HCP. With four trump, or three trump to a high honor (A, K, or Q), and some shape, you can choose to raise to 2M; with five trump, consider the preemptive 4M.
   * - Simple raise
     - 2M: |3ormore| trump, 6-10\ :sup:`-`.
   * - Limit raise, 3-card
     - 2♣! (Reverse Drury): limit raise, |3ormore| trump, forcing.
   * - Limit raise, 4-card
     - 2♣! (Reverse Drury), the same bid.
   * - Game-forcing raise
     - 2♣! Drury (the passed hand got better with the fit).
   * - Splinter
     - A double jump in a new suit, when the fit has made the hand game-going.
   * - Preemptive raise
     - 3M: weak, four trump. 4M: weak, |5ormore| trump and shortness.
   * - 1N
     - Not forcing: 6-9 HCP, no support. With 10 HCP and no fit, you may downgrade to 1N.
   * - New suit at the 1 level
     - 1♠ over 1♥: |4ormore| spades, |6ormore| HCP, not forcing.
   * - New suit at the 2 level
     - An invitational hand, not forcing. 2♣ is Drury, not natural: with only five clubs, bid 3♣ if they are good enough, otherwise 1N.
   * - Jump shift
     - 3♣ or 3♦: six cards, invitational, not forcing. For other jump shifts see :ref:`Jump_Shifts`.
   * - 2N
     - 11-12, balanced, no fit, not forcing.
   * - 3N
     - Not applicable.
   * - Double
     - Not applicable.
   * - Redouble
     - Not applicable.
   * - Cue bid of their suit
     - Not applicable.

.. list-table:: After A Takeout Double: 1M (X)
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 75

   * - Responder's action
     - Meaning
   * - Pass
     - Less than 6 HCP, not suitable for a preemptive raise.
   * - Simple raise
     - 2M, the same as without the double.
   * - Limit raise, 3-card
     - Redouble, then show the support.
   * - Limit raise, 4-card
     - 2N! (Jordan 2NT): |4ormore| trump, limit raise or better.
   * - Game-forcing raise
     - Not applicable. (Jordan 2NT is forcing.)
   * - Splinter
     - As without the double.
   * - Preemptive raise
     - 3M is weak, four trump; 4M is five or more trump.
   * - 1N
     - Not forcing, 6-9 HCP, no fit.
   * - New suit at the 1 level
     - 1♠ over 1♥: |6ormore| HCP, forcing one round; some hands with |10ormore| HCP redouble instead.
   * - New suit at the 2 level
     - 6-9, 2/1 is off, not forcing.
   * - Jump shift
     - Weak (e.g. 1♥ (X) 3♣: six clubs, weak).
   * - 2N
     - Jordan 2NT (see limit raise, 4-card).
   * - 3N
     - Not applicable.
   * - Double
     - Not applicable.
   * - Redouble
     - |10ormore| HCP, denies four trump; forcing to two of opener's suit (Going For Blood).
   * - Cue bid of their suit
     - Not applicable.

.. list-table:: After An Overcall: 1M (overcall)
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 75

   * - Responder's action
     - Meaning
   * - Pass
     - Weak hands. Also pass with a trump stack in their suit, and let opener reopen.
   * - Simple raise
     - 2M, |3ormore| trump, 6-10\ :sup:`-`; Axxx of trump and a ruffing value is also enough.
   * - Limit raise, 3-card
     - Cue bid of their suit: limit raise or better.
   * - Limit raise, 4-card
     - The same cue bid. By partnership agreement, 2N when the cue bid would force to game, as in 1♥ (2♠).
   * - Game-forcing raise
     - Cue bid, then game. 1♥ (2♠) 3♠ is game forcing.
   * - Splinter
     - A jump cue bid.
   * - Preemptive raise
     - A jump raise to 3M is preemptive, usually four trump. 4M: weak, |5ormore| trump.
   * - 1N
     - 1♥ (1♠) 1N only: typically shows a spade stopper but not enough values for 2N, say 8-9 HCP.
   * - New suit at the 1 level
     - Not applicable.
   * - New suit at the 2 level
     - |10ormore| points and a five-card suit. Forcing, but not game forcing.
   * - Jump shift
     - Weak (preemptive).
   * - 2N
     - Natural: invitational, balanced, a stopper in their suit. Can be passed.
   * - 3N
     - To play, with stoppers in their suit.
   * - Double
     - Negative double (through 2♠): 6 points at the one level, 8 to 10 at the two level.
   * - Redouble
     - Not applicable.
   * - Cue bid of their suit
     - Limit raise or better in opener's major. A jump cue bid is a splinter.

   :header-rows: 1

     - Meaning
   * - Pass

   * - Limit raise or better

   * - Jump to 4m
     - Slam try (a double jump): seriously interested in 6m.

     - 6-10, natural, not forcing; need not be balanced. Also with exactly 10 and no major or fit.

   * - New suit at the 2 level

   * - Jump shift

   * - 2N
     - 11-12\ :sup:`-`, no four-card major, invitational.
   * - 3N
     - Balanced, |13ormore| HCP, game forcing, stoppers in the other suits, no four-card major.
   * - Double
     - Not applicable.
   * - Redouble
     - Not applicable.
